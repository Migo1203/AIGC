## 产品描述初稿

Meet Eric, a compact rechargeable table lamp with a rounded shade and a softly understated presence. Move it from a bedside table to a dining setting, then turn the top knob to adjust the brightness. Its powder-coated aluminum body and small footprint make it an easy addition to a range of spaces.

The NED product page lists a 2,700K–4,000K color temperature range, up to 8 hours of runtime depending on brightness, and an approximately 6-hour charging time. A charging cord is included, while a power adapter is not included. Product details and included components should be verified for the target US version before publication.
