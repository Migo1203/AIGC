2. **ADJUST THE MOOD:** Press the top knob to switch the lamp on or off, turn it to dim from 5% to 100%, and press and turn to adjust color temperature from 2,700K to 4,000K. The lamp is rated at 270 lumens.*

## 产品描述初稿

Meet Eric, a compact rechargeable table lamp with a rounded shade and a softly understated presence. Move it from a bedside table to a dining setting, then turn the top knob to adjust the brightness. Its powder-coated aluminum body and small footprint make it an easy addition to a range of spaces.

The lamp offers a 2,700K–4,000K color temperature range, up to 8 hours of runtime depending on brightness, and an approximately 6-hour charging time. A charging cord is included; a power adapter is not included.
